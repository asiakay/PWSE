How to use this model

Identify the "choke point" in your policy goal. If your score is low, look at the denominator. Often, activists spend too much energy on the numerator when the real friction is in the Alternative Frames.

The "Denominator" Trap

Reducing Opposition Power from 10 to 5 is mathematically identical to doubling your entire numerator. Sometimes the best strategy is to peel away the opposition's allies rather than adding more of your own.
